        About & limitations
        
    
          
  • Works today: sticky add, auto store, trip-ordered groupings, check-off, subtotals, map links, deal chips, filter/sort, export/share, offline list.
    • 
          
  • Needs you: update data/deals.csv from open weekly ads you may copy; Actions rebuilds deals.json.
    • 
          
  • We do not scrape: Instacart, GasBuddy, proprietary apps, or ToS-violating sources.
    • 
          
  • “AI”: honest rules engine + optional user-supplied free LLM. No custom-trained model.
