About & limitations

        
    
          
  • What works today: shopping list, algorithmic store assignment, list-by-store, export/share, local store catalog, seed deal board, tips, optional browser LLM key.
    • 
          
  • What needs you: update data/deals.csv from open weekly ads / circulars you are allowed to copy, then let GitHub Actions rebuild deals.json.
    • 
          
  • We do not scrape: Instacart, GasBuddy, proprietary store apps, or anything that violates ToS. Flipp FlyerKit needs a partner token — not used here.
    • 
          
  • “AI”: honest rules engine + optional user-supplied free LLM. No custom-trained model is claimed or shipped.
    • 
        

        
See README.md and CONTRIBUTING.md in the repo.
